  • Grid: 12 × 6 cells. Speed 5×6, Battery 3×3, Range 3×3, Power, Temperature and Sag 4×2 each. This is the default preset for 1 controller.
  • Speed: radial gauge with a 250° sweep. The scale max rounds the vehicle's top speed up to the next 20. A white tick marks the ride's max. The chip above the number shows where speed comes from (VESC / ERPM / GPS).
  • Status strip: link state, measured poll rate, GPS, page dots, the REC pill (tap to toggle logging, shows ride time) and the clock.
  • Power bar: regen goes left of the zero line (teal), drive goes right (amber). The thin white line is the ride's peak.
  • Temperature bars are scaled to the controller's derating start. Colour follows the zones: ok, then warn at 10 °C before derating, then crit.
  • Rule: live numbers use the display face with tabular digits, at least 28 dp. Labels are 11 dp uppercase.

  • Only with 2+ controllers. With one controller this page and every source selector are hidden.
  • Controller card: one per controller, named by the user (default Front/Rear on a scooter). The chip shows the link: Local or CAN id.

  • AlertBanner slides in from the top, over the status strip. Critical alerts stay until the condition clears or the rider mutes them. Warnings auto-hide after 6 s.
