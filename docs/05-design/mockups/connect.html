A bridge talks to one app at a time. Close VESC Tool first.

  • Order: known devices first, then by signal. The subtitle names the detected bridge type (Nordic UART, VESC Express, generic FFE0 UART).
  • Tapping a row connects at once. There is no separate Connect button.
  • With "Show all" off, only devices with a known UART service appear. A device without one stays disabled and shows why.
  • Permissions are asked here the first time (nearby devices, notifications), with one sentence on why.

  • Detection runs on every connect. This screen only appears the first time, or when the set of controllers changed.
  • Names: one controller is called "Motor". Two on a scooter default to Front/Rear. Everything is editable later in Vehicle.
  • Non-motor CAN nodes (BMS, IO boards, VESC Express) are listed so the rider sees them, but they are never polled for motor values.
  • With one controller the list has one row and the screen closes by itself after 1.5 s.
